## Numerals: Arabic-Indic

The paper prints its numbers in Arabic-Indic digits (٠١٢٣٤٥٦٧٨٩), and so will the
figure. **You write every number in Western digits 0–9 anyway** — a label, a
tick, a value, an axis limit, a coordinate — with «.» as the decimal point and
`\%` for per cent. The paper renders every printed digit as the book prints it,
in the text of a node and in the labels an `axis` prints by itself alike, and a
digit written as ٠–٩ in the picture is one TikZ cannot compute with and the
typesetter cannot place.

The description was written under the same rule, so its digits are already 0–9:
copy them as they are.
