## Language: Arabic

The book is in Arabic, and so is everything you write: overview, headings, stems, exercises, choices, answers, glossary, teaching notes, and figure descriptions. Write Modern Standard Arabic in the register of the book itself.

- Use the book's own terms exactly as the attached Arabic PDFs print them. Never replace a term with a synonym from another Arabic curriculum, and never gloss it in English.
- Use Arabic punctuation as the book does: the comma «،», the semicolon «؛», the question mark «؟»; the full stop stays «.».
- A sentence ends with «.»; a question ends with «؟».
- The four choices of a multiple-choice exercise are printed as «(أ)», «(ب)», «(ج)» and «(د)», and `answer` names the correct one by that letter: `أ`, `ب`, `ج` or `د`.
- **Tashkeel is CONTEXTUAL on printed wording.** Write stems, exercises, choices and similar
  fields without harakāt by default; add tashkeel only on words that would be misread without
  it. Do not vocalise whole sentences. A Quranic quote in ﴿…﴾ keeps its source marks; do not
  vocalise the surrounding prose to match it. When marks are present: fatḥatān before alif
  (`نطقًا`, not `نطقا` / `نطقاً`); nisba + tanween with shadda (`شرعيًّا`, not `شرعيًا`).
- Write text in logical order, exactly as it is read. Do not insert direction marks or bidi control characters (U+200E, U+200F, U+202A–U+202E, U+2066–U+2069).
- A word the book prints in Latin letters — a proper name, an abbreviation — stays in Latin letters.
- `$...$` is only for mathematics. An Arabic word that is not a variable, a point name,
  or part of a formula stays outside `$...$`: write `افهم`, never `$افهم$`. An English
  word option stays a word in Arabic, not a formula.
- An Arabic word that *does* stand inside a formula — a unit, a function name, a
  multi-letter abbreviation such as مح — goes in `\text{...}` inside the `$...$`:
  `$5\text{ سم}$`, `$\text{مح} = 48\text{ سم}$`. Never leave that word as bare letters
  in maths (`$مح$`, `$افهم$`): the paper prints those as isolated glyphs, not as a word.
- JSON keys, ids and the fixed values of role, type and reuse stay exactly as the schema gives them, in English.
