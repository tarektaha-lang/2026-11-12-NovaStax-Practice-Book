## Numerals: Arabic-Indic

The book prints its numbers in Arabic-Indic digits (٠١٢٣٤٥٦٧٨٩), with «٫» as the decimal point
and «٪» for per cent, and so will the paper. **You write every number in Western digits 0–9
anyway**, in prose and in mathematics alike, in every field, with «.» as the decimal point and `\%` for per cent. The paper is set from your fields and renders every digit as the book prints it; a digit written as ٠–٩ in a field is one the checks cannot read and the typesetter cannot place.
