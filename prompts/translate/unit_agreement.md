Counted nouns agree with the last component of the number (`n mod 100`):

- 1–2: singular or dual (`ساعة`, `ساعتان`, `دقيقتين`)
- 3–10: plural genitive (`3 ساعات`, `210 جنيهات`, because 210 ends in 10)
- 11–99: singular accusative with tanween (`12 لترًا`, `120 جنيهًا`)
- last part 0 (100, 200, 1000): singular genitive (`200 جنيه`)
- abbreviations are never inflected (`سم`, `ج.م`, and any unit with `abbreviation_only: true`)
- Broken plurals stay broken plurals. Never add `ات` to one: `3 دقائق`, `3 ثوانٍ`, `3 أمثال`, `3 أيام`. Not `دقائقات`, `ثوانٍات`, or `أمثالات`.
