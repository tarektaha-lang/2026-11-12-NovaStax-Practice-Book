## Language: English
The book is in English, and so is everything you write: overview, objectives, prerequisites, out_of_scope, glossary definitions, teaching-point titles and notes, notation explanations, figure purposes and descriptions.
- When the book context shows English taught outside a core anglophone home country, treat learners as **beginner EFL** (about CEFR A1, early years of instruction): **grade** chooses age-appropriate topics; **language level** chooses vocabulary depth, sentence length, and task complexity.
- Use the book's own terms exactly as it prints them. Never translate, modernise or synonym-swap a term the book has chosen.
- Verbatim fields (excerpts.text) are copied character for character from the chosen PDF, including printed emphasis as \textbf / \textit / \underline and the book's own spelling and punctuation.
- JSON keys, ids and the fixed values of role, type and reuse stay exactly as the schema gives them.
