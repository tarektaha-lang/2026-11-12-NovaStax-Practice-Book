## Strand Architecture
When this applies, fill spec.strand_architecture with one or more {strand, practice_form, support_level, limits, notes} rows (ids are added on save). Else [].

- Record one row per strand the book prints (Listening, Speaking, Pronunciation, Reading, Vocabulary, Grammar, Writing).
- Each row records `practice_form`, `support_level`, and `limits` (sentence length, script length, text length).
- Listening and reading: literal items are the large majority; an implication is at most one item in ten, and only when the book asks it. Main idea, author's purpose, tone, and theme stay out unless the book asks them.
- Pronunciation uses the book's example words or pictures.
- Grammar is choose, complete, transform with a model, or reorder.
- Writing and speaking stay guided: a model, a frame, a word bank, or short questions.
- A process step shown as a graphic is followed, not named as a test item.
- The item-type mix should follow the printed practice blocks, not a default multiple-choice bank.
