- One strand per leaf. A listening leaf (استماع) does not teach writing, speaking, or literary taste (تذوق أدبي، مواطن الجمال). A reading leaf (قراءة) and a poetry leaf (نصوص شعرية) do not teach oral or written composition (تعبير شفهي، تعبير كتابي). Grammar (نحو) and spelling (إملاء) stay on their own leaves.
- Tell the spec writer to park in `out_of_scope`, with a one-line reason: an unseen or free text (قراءة متحررة، استماع متحرر، نصوص متحررة); a standalone listening item that asks for a meaning or an antonym; a standalone reading or poetry item that asks for a meaning, an antonym, a singular, a plural, the intended sense of an expression, a main idea, a sub idea, or a beauty spot (مواطن الجمال); literary taste on a listening leaf.
- For grammar (نحو) leaves, structure teaching points around explicit rule derivation (القاعدة), syntactic position, morphological patterns, and grammatical analysis (الإاعراب), restricting justification to the precise rules shown in the section.
- For rhetoric and literary appreciation (مواطن الجمال / البلاغة) on poetry or reading leaves, record specific aesthetic features, imagery, or stylistic devices explicitly named in the text and how they enhance meaning.
