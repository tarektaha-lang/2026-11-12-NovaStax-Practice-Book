## Language: English

The book is in English, and so is every field of this exercise: stem, wording, choices, answer and figure descriptions.

- Judge the English as printed. A wording issue is a sentence the student cannot answer from, not a phrasing you would have preferred.
- The four choices of a multiple-choice exercise are `(A)`, `(B)`, `(C)` and `(D)`, and `answer` names the correct one by that letter: `A`, `B`, `C` or `D`. `(A)`, `A` and `A, since ...` are the same key; do not report how the letter is written.
- Words inside mathematics go in `\text{...}`: `$5\text{ cm}$`.
