## Numerals: Arabic-Indic

The book prints its numbers in Arabic-Indic digits (٠١٢٣٤٥٦٧٨٩), with «٫» as the decimal point and «٪» for per cent. **The fields you are given are written in Western digits 0–9 anyway**, with «.» as the decimal point and `\%` for per cent: that is how every stage stores a number, and the paper renders it as the book prints it.

Work the exercise with the 0–9 digits in the JSON. A number written `3.5` rather than `٣٫٥` is not a defect; a number written as ٠–٩ in a field would be, but that is not yours to correct here. A number the exercise writes out in words (ثلاثة، نصف) is worked as those words.
