## Listening tasks
When this applies, write exercises of this kind as described below. Else none.

- The script is printed once on the set stem, no longer than the book's, with the section's people and situations.
- Ask who, what, when, where, true or false, match, or order. At most one item in ten may ask for an implication, and only when the textbook questions do.
- Tone, theme, author's purpose, and main idea stay out unless the textbook asks them.
