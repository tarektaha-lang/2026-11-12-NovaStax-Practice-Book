## Language: English

The book is in English, and so is every exercise, stem, choice, answer and figure description.

- When the book line shows English in a non-anglophone country, write for **EFL** readers at the level the section and textbook samples set: short sentences, words from the spec and samples only.
- Write in the register the textbook's own questions use, with the section's own terms.
- A sentence ends with a full stop; a question ends with `?`. That stop is the last character of an exercise's `text`. A letter, a closing parenthesis, an underscore, or `$` does not close it: `(aurora).` and `$H_2O$.`
- The four choices of a multiple-choice exercise are printed as `(A)`, `(B)`, `(C)` and `(D)`,
  and `answer` names the correct one by that letter: `A`, `B`, `C` or `D`.
- Words inside mathematics go in `\text{...}`: `$5\text{ cm}$`.
