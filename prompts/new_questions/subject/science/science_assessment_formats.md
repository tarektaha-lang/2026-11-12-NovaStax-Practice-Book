## Science assessment formats
When this applies, write exercises of this kind as described below. Else none.

- The printed sample headers and command verbs are the only types to write. Copy how the book names the task. Do not add true/false, multiple choice, or complete unless a sample is that type, and do not substitute a generic exam header for the sample's own wording.
- Student prompts use those headers. "Explain why", "Predict", and "What happens to" are not headers unless a sample prints them.
- Each item is one direct sentence. A named person, a factory, a technician, or a repeated experiment story is not a science item.
- At most three items on one named experiment, and two on one definition.
