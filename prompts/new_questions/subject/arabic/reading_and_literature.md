## Reading and literature
When this applies, write exercises of this kind as described below. Else none.

- This block covers reading (قراءة) and poetry (نصوص شعرية). Use only the prescribed passage or poem, printed once on the set stem. Do not write an unseen text (قراءة متحررة، نصوص متحررة).
- Do not write speaking or writing tasks in this block.
- A standalone item has no passage and no verse. It tests general comprehension or a higher skill only. It does not ask for a meaning, an antonym, a singular, a plural, the intended sense of an expression, a main idea, a sub idea, or a beauty spot.
- Those jobs appear only as children of a set. Sets are the majority of a reading or poetry objective.
- In one reading or poetry set, at most one child asks for vocabulary in context. Most children are comprehension. At most two children are higher-order. Beauty or rhetoric may be one of those higher-order children.
