## Functional and creative writing
When this applies, write exercises of this kind as described below. Else none.

- Apply this block only on a writing leaf (تعبير كتابي) or a speaking leaf (تحدث). Never on listening, reading, or poetry.
- A writing item is either one essay that asks the student to produce the target pattern, or a set whose stem prints a model of that pattern and whose children analyse its parts.
