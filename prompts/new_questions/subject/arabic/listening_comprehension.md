## Listening comprehension
When this applies, write exercises of this kind as described below. Else none.

- Use only the prescribed script stored on the section spec, printed once on the set stem. Do not write an unseen listening text (استماع متحرر). The stem says the text is heard (استمع / النص المسموع).
- Do not write speaking, writing, literary taste (تذوق أدبي), or beauty (مواطن الجمال) items on a listening objective.
- A standalone item has no script in `text` or `stem`. It does not ask for a meaning or an antonym. It asks general comprehension or a higher skill.
- In one listening set, at most one child asks for vocabulary in context. Most children are comprehension. At most two children are higher-order.
