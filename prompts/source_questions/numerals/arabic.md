## Numerals: Arabic-Indic

The book prints its numbers in Arabic-Indic digits (٠١٢٣٤٥٦٧٨٩), with «٫» as the decimal
point and «٪» for per cent. **Transcribe every number in Western digits 0–9 anyway**, in
prose and in mathematics alike, with «.» as the decimal point and `\%` for per cent: «٣٫٥»
is written `3.5`, «٢٥٪» is written `25\%`, and a thousands separator «٬» is dropped. The
paper is set from your fields and renders every digit as the book prints it, and a digit
written as ٠–٩ in a field is one the checks cannot read and the typesetter cannot place.

This is the one place faithful transcription changes a character. Everything else about
a number stays as printed: its position in the sentence, the operators around it, whether
the book wrote it in digits or in words (ثلاثة، نصف stay as words).
