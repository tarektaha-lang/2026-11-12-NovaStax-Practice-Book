## Language: Arabic
The section is in Arabic and so is every heading, in Modern Standard Arabic in the register of the section itself.
- A heading is a noun phrase or a masdar phrase — «جمع الكسور المتشابهة», «إثبات تطابق المثلثات» — never a full sentence, and never a question.
- Count words as the six-word limit counts them: each space-separated word is one, so «الأعداد العشرية وترتيبها» is three.
- No tashkeel unless a heading is ambiguous without it.
- Write in logical order with no direction marks or bidi control characters.
- Where an objective is about a symbol or a letter, name it in Arabic words («الجذر التربيعي», «المجهول»), never as the symbol, never in Latin letters.
- Numbers that belong in a heading are written in words where the section would («قوى العشرة»); digits only where the section itself uses them as a name, and then as Western digits 0–9, which the paper renders as the book prints them.
